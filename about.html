Welcome!

Plasticine Games is a small independent game studio owned and operated by Shawn.Genji. What started as a hobby has grown into a passion project dedicated to creating relaxing puzzle games, tactile idle experiments, and experiences that are polished, thoughtfully designed, and simply fun to play.

Every game is designed, programmed, and maintained in spare time, with attention to the details that make the biggest difference: responsive controls, handcrafted visuals, carefully chosen sound effects, and music that ties the whole experience together. The goal is simple — games worth coming back to.

Your Privacy

Each game stores a small amount of information locally on your device using your browser’s local storage — things like progress, settings, and personal accomplishments. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to Plasticine Games.

Plasticine Games games do not include player accounts, cloud saves, or tracking technologies of our own. Some sites may display advertisements through third-party providers such as Google AdSense or Adsterra, which may collect information under their own privacy policies. See each game’s Privacy Policy for details.
